Context Engineering in RAG

After retrieval, the system prepares context for the LLM.

This step is called context engineering.

Key tasks:
- Selecting relevant chunks
- Removing duplicates
- Compressing content
- Ordering information

Why it matters:
- LLM input is limited by token size
- Too much context can reduce accuracy
- Irrelevant context leads to hallucination

Techniques:
- Context compression
- Relevance filtering
- Priority ranking

Goal:
Provide the most relevant and concise information to the LLM.
