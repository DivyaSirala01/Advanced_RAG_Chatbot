Chunking in RAG Systems

Chunking is the process of breaking large documents into smaller pieces called chunks.

Why chunking is important:
- LLMs have token limits
- Smaller chunks improve retrieval precision
- Large documents are difficult to search efficiently

Common chunking strategies:
1. Fixed-size chunking
2. Recursive chunking (preferred)
3. Semantic chunking
4. Sentence-based chunking

Key parameters:
- Chunk size
- Chunk overlap

Chunk overlap ensures continuity of context across chunks.

Example:
A document of 2000 words may be split into chunks of 500 words with 50-word overlap.

Advanced chunking techniques:
- Context-aware chunking
- Hierarchical chunking
- Adaptive chunking

Poor chunking can lead to:
- Loss of context
- Irrelevant retrieval
- Reduced accuracy
