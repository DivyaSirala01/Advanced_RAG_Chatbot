Advanced RAG Systems

Modern RAG systems go beyond basic retrieval and generation.

Advanced features include:
- Hybrid retrieval (BM25 + vector search)
- Reranking with cross-encoders
- Multi-hop reasoning
- Self-RAG (self-reflection)
- Query understanding and routing

Performance improvements:
- Semantic caching
- Response reuse
- Optimization techniques

Agentic RAG:
- Planning steps
- Tool usage
- Iterative reasoning

Challenges:
- Scalability
- Latency
- Cost optimization

Advanced RAG systems are used in:
- Enterprise AI assistants
- Legal AI
- Healthcare systems
- Developer copilots

The future of RAG lies in combining retrieval, reasoning, and decision-making.
