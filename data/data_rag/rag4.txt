Embeddings in RAG Systems

Embeddings are numerical vector representations of text.

They convert text into a format that machines can understand and compare.

Example:
"leave policy" → [0.23, -0.91, 0.45, ...]

Why embeddings are important:
- Enable semantic search
- Capture meaning, not just keywords

Types of embeddings:
- Sentence embeddings
- Document embeddings

Embedding models:
- OpenAI embeddings
- Sentence Transformers
- Cohere embeddings

Key properties:
- Similar meanings → similar vectors
- Distance between vectors represents similarity

Challenges:
- Choosing the right embedding model
- Handling domain-specific vocabulary

Embeddings are stored in a vector database for fast retrieval.
