Retrieval in RAG Systems

Retrieval is the process of finding relevant chunks from the knowledge base based on a user query.

Steps:
1. Convert query into embedding
2. Perform similarity search
3. Retrieve top-k relevant chunks

Types of retrieval:
- Dense retrieval (vector-based)
- Sparse retrieval (BM25)
- Hybrid retrieval (combining both)

Top-k selection determines how many chunks are retrieved.

Challenges:
- Irrelevant results
- Missing important context
- Over-retrieval

Improving retrieval:
- Query rewriting
- Multi-query expansion
- Reranking

Retrieval quality directly impacts final answer quality.
