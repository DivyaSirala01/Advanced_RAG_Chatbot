Planning in Agentic Systems

Planning is the core intelligence of an agent.

Part 1: Task Decomposition

The agent breaks a complex task into smaller steps.

Example:
Task: "Summarize and analyze document"

Steps:
1. Extract text
2. Identify key sections
3. Summarize
4. Analyze risks

Part 2: Planning Strategies

Types of planning:
- Static planning (fixed steps)
- Dynamic planning (adaptive)
- Iterative planning (refines over time)

Good planning reduces errors and improves efficiency.
