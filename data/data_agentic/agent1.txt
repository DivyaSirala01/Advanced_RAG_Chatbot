Introduction to Agentic AI Systems

Agentic AI refers to systems where AI models can autonomously make decisions, plan actions, and execute tasks instead of just responding to prompts.

Unlike traditional LLMs that follow a single input-output pattern, agentic systems operate in loops and can adapt their behavior based on intermediate results.

Part 1: What is an Agent?

An agent is an AI system that:
- Receives input
- Decides what to do
- Takes actions
- Observes outcomes
- Adjusts its behavior

This makes agents capable of solving complex, multi-step problems.

Part 2: Why Agentic Workflows?

Agentic workflows are useful when:
- Tasks require multiple steps
- Decisions depend on intermediate results
- External tools are needed
- The system must adapt dynamically

Examples:
- Booking systems
- Code generation pipelines
- Legal document analysis
- Autonomous assistants
